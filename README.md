# Savora AI — The Intelligence Behind Every Meal 🍽️
https://lovable.dev/preview/4PdKUlDxZMLiU75Et9lZfv71nzzE0QXP

**Savora AI** is an intelligent kitchen assistant designed to make everyday cooking smarter, easier, and more efficient.

It aims to help users locate stored kitchen items, identify available ingredients, discover personalized recipes, and reduce food waste through AI-powered kitchen management.

## 💡 Problem Statement

People often struggle to remember where ingredients are stored, keep track of available food, decide what to cook, and use ingredients before they expire. Savora AI aims to address these everyday challenges through a unified intelligent assistant.

## 🚀 Key Features

- **Smart Item Locator:** Helps users find kitchen essentials by remembering their storage locations.
- **AI Ingredient Recognition:** Identifies vegetables and other ingredients from images.
- **Personalized Recipe Suggestions:** Recommends meals based on available ingredients and user preferences.
- **Smart Inventory Management:** Tracks ingredient quantities and storage locations.
- **Expiry-Aware Suggestions:** Helps prioritize ingredients that should be used sooner.
- **Voice Assistance:** Envisions hands-free kitchen interaction through compatible voice platforms.
- **Food Waste Reduction:** Encourages users to make better use of ingredients they already have.

## ⚙️ How It Works

1. Users provide information about their kitchen items and ingredients.
2. The assistant helps locate stored items or identify ingredients.
3. AI-powered recommendations suggest suitable meals.
4. Inventory information can be updated as ingredients are used.
5. Expiry-aware suggestions help users plan meals more efficiently.

## 🎯 Our Goal

Our goal is to transform the traditional kitchen into an intelligent, organized, and resource-efficient environment where AI simplifies everyday decisions.

## 🔮 Future Enhancements

The technology stack will depend on the implementation and may include AI models, image recognition, a database, a web interface, and compatible APIs.

Savora AI aims to save time, simplify meal planning, improve kitchen organization, and reduce avoidable food waste.
